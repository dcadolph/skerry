Privacy

Effective 30 July 2026

Skerry collects nothing. It has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. It does not collect, receive, store, or transmit your content, your encryption key, or any personal information. There is no backend to send anything to.

What we collect

Nothing. We hold no data about you, so there is none to disclose, sell, share, or hand over, and none to lose in a breach.

What stays on your device

Everything. Your content and your keys remain on hardware you own, or on storage you choose and control. We are not a party to that, and we never see the data or the keys.

Tracking

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising.
  • No third-party components that collect data.
  • No cookies and no device fingerprinting.

Children

Skerry gathers no personal information from anyone of any age. It is not directed at children.

Changes

If Skerry ever offers an optional hosted service, this policy will be updated to describe exactly what that service does before it ships. Any such service will be end to end encrypted, so that we still cannot read your content.
